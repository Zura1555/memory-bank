### Per-claim survival correction after the third qualification (new candidate, not yet paid-qualified)

Offline replay of the preserved paid outputs isolated a shared server-side cause behind several total losses, distinct from model variance: `buildCheckedAssessment` and `buildCheckedBrief` required **every** claim in an artifact's or item's set (`keepAll`) to be accepted before any of it survived, and the server-generated `classification-disposition-*` label depended on every local claim, so dependency closure transmitted one peripheral withheld claim into the disposition itself. In the third qualification's H2 assessment, Cedar mapping's disposition and classification verdicts were both `supported`, yet the work was nulled because one verification claim was `unsupported`. Dependency closure already withholds any claim that actually depends on a withheld claim, and every surviving claim is independently judged supported, so the whole-set rule added brittleness without adding evidence.

The correction is claim-by-claim survival in both builders, plus a truthful dependency for the generated label:

- Artifact work survives iff its disposition claim and its classification claim are accepted (role rejection still rejects the disposition claim itself); surviving claim/blocker/verification lists are filtered to accepted claims only. Brief items survive iff their disposition claim is accepted, with claim lists filtered the same way. Outcome classifications, lead/contrast reasons and comparison rows keep their whole-unit rule, and the brief coverage check still rejects every recommendation of an item missing a required quotation, so quotation failures still remove the item.
- `classification-disposition-*` now depends on the disposition recommendation alone — the only claim its label actually restates. Its independent review verdict is unchanged.

Preserved-output replay (no provider calls) through the corrected code proves both directions: the third qualification's H2 draft/review now yields Cedar mapping as Adapt lead work with only the unsupported verification claim withheld; the first qualification's H2 brief draft/review now yields the selected mapping item with only the cross-operation recovery claim withheld; and the third qualification's P3 checklist — whose disposition claim itself was `unsupported` — **remains withheld**, so the fix resurrects nothing the reviewer actually rejected.
